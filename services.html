For more than twenty years, BlueLine Plumbing has delivered honest, reliable plumbing work to families and businesses across the region. Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do you offer free estimates?

    Yes — we provide a free, no-obligation estimate on every service call before any work begins. You'll know the flat price up front.

  • What areas do you serve?

    We cover the entire metro area, including Springfield, Riverside, Oak Park, Maplewood, and dozens of surrounding towns. Call us with your zip code if you're unsure.

  • Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

    Every plumber on our team holds a current state license and is fully insured and bonded. Our company license number is displayed on every invoice and in the footer of this site.

  • Do you charge for after-hours service?

    Emergency and after-hours calls carry a modest dispatch fee, which is always quoted before we head out. There are no surprise charges once we arrive.

  • What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept all major credit cards, debit, checks, and cash. Financing is available on larger jobs — ask your technician for details.
